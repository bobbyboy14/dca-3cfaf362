Working it out…
RankFrom 52-week highvs your costMayer (price ÷ 200-day)Score
How the score works (all in A$, higher = more beaten down): (a) Fall from 52-week high: a 30% fall scores 30 points. (b) Below your own average cost: 10% under your cost scores 10 points, 10% over scores −10. SEMI isn't held, so it has no (b). (c) Value check: a Mayer multiple of 0.90 (10% under the 200-day average) scores 10 points, 1.10 scores −10. Score = the average of the parts each asset has. Recalculated every time you open the page or press Refresh, with Bitcoin live from CoinGecko.
Keep in mind
This is a mechanical value indicator, not advice. The most beaten-down holding isn't always the best buy, because prices often fall for a reason. MSTR is a leveraged Bitcoin proxy, so it falls harder than Bitcoin and will often top this list when Bitcoin is weak.

The maths, step by step

Step 1: money in A$3,393 received on 18 Jul 2022 + A$14,118.50 across 45 buys = A$17,511.50 Step 2: money out 8 sales, including A$31,124 on 7 Nov 2025 and A$22,614 on 20 Nov 2025 = A$60,914.62 Step 3: time held 18 Jul 2022 to 20 Nov 2025 = 1,221 days ÷ 365.25 = 3.34 years Step 4: multiple 60,914.62 ÷ 17,511.50 = 3.48x Step 5: simple CAGR 3.48(1 ÷ 3.34) − 1 ≈ 45.2% a year. This treats all the money as if it went in on day one. Step 6: money-weighted return (XIRR) ≈ 62.8% a year. This is the fairer measure, because your buys were spread out over time, so much of the money was invested for less than 3.34 years.
