Tesla (TSLA) price, US$

Blue line, projected price: where Tesla could go at the growth rate you set above (25% every six months by default).
Yellow line, break-even: your average cost per share as you keep buying. Above it you're in profit.
Red dashed line, target: the 2030 price you're aiming for. If the blue line ends above it, you're on track.
Green line, rolling trend: where Tesla ends up if it keeps growing at its recent blended pace. The pace mixes the last 6 months (25%), last 12 months (50%) and last 3 years (25%). It's recalculated from real prices every Sunday, so it moves as the trend changes.
Pink dashed line, your custom rate: where Tesla would be if it grew at "Your growth rate" set in the Tesla panel on the left. It starts on the trend rate until you move it.
