How it works: prices grow by the semi-annual rate every six months, compounding smoothly week to week, from the start date to 31 Dec 2030. Your existing shares and any one-time deposit go in on the start date, and each week's DCA buys at that week's projected price. The break-even line is your average cost per share, so when the price line is above it, that position is ahead of what you've put in. In the merged scenario, one current Tesla share is worth about US$2,935 (about 2.96 merged shares at US$990), and one SpaceX share is one merged share at about US$990. All figures are in US$ before fees and currency conversion. These targets are one analyst's bull-case scenario, not a forecast. This is for illustration only, not financial advice.
